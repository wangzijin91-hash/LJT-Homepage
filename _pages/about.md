I am Junteng Liu, a first-year Ph.D. candidate at the **HKUST NLP Group** within the **Hong Kong University of Science and Technology (HKUST)**, where I am pursuing my Ph.D. in Computer Science (2024-Present). I am privileged to be supervised by **Professor Junxian He**. 

Previously, I completed my B.Eng. in Computer Science (2020-2024) at **Shanghai Jiao Tong University (SJTU)**, graduating in June 2024. During my undergraduate studies at SJTU, I was awarded the prestigious **Zhiyuan Honor Scholarship** and was also advised by Professor Junxian He.

Research Interests
======
My research focuses on natural language processing and machine learning. More specifically, my interests include:
*   **LLM Reasoning and Reinforcement Learning**
*   **Hallucination in Vision-Language Models (VLM)**
*   **LLM Truthfulness and Interpretability**
